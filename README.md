# Data Science in E-Retail

Coursework for Data Science in E-Retail at Cornell Tech, Fall 2026.

## Skills showcased

- **Discrete-event simulation from scratch:** a future event list, exponential interarrival and service times, and single- and two-server queues.
- **Simulation statistics:** tracking time-weighted measures such as time in system and server utilization.
- **Simulating a network:** customers traveling between randomly chosen origin-destination pairs over a simulated day.
- **Dynamic programming for revenue management:** the optimal price for limited inventory over a fixed number of periods, with random arrivals and random willingness to pay.
- **Choice-based pricing:** pricing several products at once under the multinomial logit (MNL) model.
- **Tools:** Python, Jupyter, NumPy.
